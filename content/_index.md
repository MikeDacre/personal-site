# Hi, I'm Mike Dacre.

I'm a family medicine physician in California. I look after whole people, not
single problems, and I try to make every visit feel unhurried.

This site is one place for two things: a plain description of how I practice,
for patients and anyone thinking of becoming one, and my professional
background, for colleagues and employers.

If you're a patient, start with [how I practice](/practice/). If you're a
colleague or recruiter, my [CV](/cv/) and [publications](/publications/) are
the fastest route. Either way, you can [get in touch](/contact/).
