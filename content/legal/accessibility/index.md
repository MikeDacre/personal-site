I want everyone to be able to use this site, including people who rely on
screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification, or reduced motion.

This site **aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines
(WCAG) 2.1, Level AA**. In practice that means, among other things: text with
sufficient color contrast, full keyboard operability, visible focus
indicators, semantic headings, described images, and layouts that work on a
small phone screen and at 200% zoom.

The site is under active development, and a full conformance audit will be
completed before launch. If you hit a barrier anywhere on this site, please
[contact me](/contact/) — I will treat it as a bug and fix it.
