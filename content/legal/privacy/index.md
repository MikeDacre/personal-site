This is a personal website. It is built to collect as little about you as
possible.

**No analytics.** This site uses no analytics, no cookies, no tracking pixels,
no session recording, and no fingerprinting of any kind.

**Hosting.** The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes network-level
information (such as your IP address) to deliver pages and protect against
abuse, as any web host does. I don't see or keep that information.

**Contact form (coming soon).** When the contact form is added, messages will
travel by email and will not be stored by this website. The form page will
explain exactly how it works before you use it. Please never include medical
details or personal health information — the form is not a secure or
confidential channel.

If anything on this page stops being accurate, that is a bug — please
[tell me](/contact/).
